# Working together

Help people improve their farming workflows. Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome different levels of farming, GIS and programming experience. Give credit, explain decisions and discuss the work without personal attacks, harassment or discrimination.

Respect confidentiality: do not disclose another person's farm/customer data, identity or private communications. Raise conduct concerns privately with admin@agtechandmachine.com. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or decline work, and should explain their decisions where doing so does not expose private information. No guaranteed response time is implied.
